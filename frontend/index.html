Your AI Manager Builds a Fantasy XI, One Gameweek at a Time

A trained reinforcement-learning agent (PPO) sets the strategy — how aggressive to be with transfers, how much budget to spend, whether to play a chip — and a constraint solver (MILP) turns that strategy into the actual legal squad, starting XI, and captain. Every gameweek is built on top of the previous gameweek's real result: transfers, bank, free transfers, and chip usage all carry forward, exactly like a real manager's season.

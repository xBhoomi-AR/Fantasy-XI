A sequential, AI-assisted Fantasy Premier League manager

This demo runs a real, trained reinforcement-learning agent (PPO) on top of a constraint solver (MILP) across a real historical Premier League season, one gameweek at a time. Each gameweek's squad is built on top of the previous gameweek's actual result — transfers, bank, free transfers, and chip usage all carry forward, exactly like a real manager's season.

PPO does not pick your 15 players directly. It chooses a small strategic setting (how aggressive to be with transfers, how much budget to spend, whether to play a chip) which is then handed to a MILP solver that selects the actual legal squad, starting XI, and captain.
